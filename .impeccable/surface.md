# CiteRight application direction

Mode: Operate. Students work at laptops in daylight and on phones between classes; use a light default following system preference, with an equally readable dark mode.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A playful book workspace that turns citation chores into a short, clear search-and-review flow.
OWN-WORLD: Violet identity, mint and butter-yellow geometric book objects, pale lavender canvas, rounded controls, Manrope lettering, precise dark ink.
STORY: Find a book, choose the exact edition, check its details, cite it and place it in a personal library.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact top navigation above a large two-line title, a substantial search control, example searches, and a composed animated book stack to the right. Results remain the main task below the hero.
FORM: User-approved playful digital library. Code-led implementation. The concept-seed launcher was attempted but its engine could not run in the sandbox; the explicit user direction is the design authority.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
