# CiteRight

A free, open-source book and research paper citation workspace for students, researchers, and academic writers. React/TypeScript/Vite with Tailwind and shadcn/ui; static Vercel and local npm deployment. No accounts, database, paid APIs, or secrets.

Research paper extension: DOI/DOI-link lookup through Crossref for journal articles and papers published in conference proceedings, appropriate editable metadata, APA/MLA formatting, and mixed libraries/exports. Google Scholar is a discovery source: users copy a paper's DOI from its publisher page. Scholar links, PDF extraction, preprints, theses, and unpublished conference presentations are outside this feature.

Search Open Library, explicitly choose an edition, review editable metadata, generate APA 7 / MLA 9 bibliographies and parenthetical citations with page locators. Never invent missing data. Save browser-local references, organize collections, copy selected bibliographies, import/export JSON and export BibTeX. Autosave drafts. Show actionable readiness checks and optional formatting explanations.

## Platform

Web, mobile through desktop. Accessibility and reduced motion are required.

## Approved design

Playful digital library with violet, mint and yellow, animated geometric books, result entrances, and a book-to-library save interaction. Calmer citation editor. User explicitly requested all improvements. No marketing statistics or invented accuracy claims.

## Delivery

Deployment-ready source only. Source URL remains unconfigured until publication. No actual GitHub creation or Vercel deployment requested.
