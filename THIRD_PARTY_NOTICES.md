## Runtime data

Crossref provides publisher-deposited journal article metadata at runtime through its public REST API. CiteRight does not fetch or redistribute article full text or abstracts. See [Crossref's metadata retrieval documentation](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/) and [access and usage guidance](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/access-and-authentication/). Source publishers retain rights in their own content; Crossref metadata availability does not grant access to paywalled papers.

Open Library / Internet Archive provide metadata and cover images at runtime. These are external resources, not assets created or owned by CiteRight. See [Open Library's licensing guidance](https://openlibrary.org/developers/licensing) and its [API policy](https://openlibrary.org/developers/api).
